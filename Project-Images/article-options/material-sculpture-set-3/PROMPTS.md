# Material Sculpture Set 3

Generated September 9, 2026 as a third 20-image exploration. These are review options only; none is selected for publication.

## Shared direction

- 3:2 abstract editorial cover
- Photoreal studio photography of real fabricated sculpture
- Cast, woven, compressed, eroded, hinged and counterbalanced construction logic
- Black seamless background; charcoal, warm bone and restrained orange palette
- Visible joins, tolerances, material flaws, gravity, pressure and shadows
- No text, people, devices, diagrams, icons, logos, borders or watermarks
