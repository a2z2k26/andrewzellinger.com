# Material Sculpture Set 4: Geometric

Generated September 9, 2026 as a 20-image geometric continuation. Review options only; none is selected for publication.

## Shared direction

- 3:2 editorial covers
- Photoreal studio photography of physically fabricated abstract sculpture
- Crisp primitives, measured spacing, apertures, tangencies, repetition and interlocking volumes
- Real material thickness, joins, tool marks, imperfections, gravity and contact shadows
- Charcoal black, warm bone, restrained orange and occasional aged metal
- No text, people, interfaces, diagrams, icons, logos, borders or watermarks
